About us

Shuxrat oʻgʻli is the brand of Baraka Hamkor Parranda, a private enterprise.

Shuxrat oʻgʻli is the brand of Baraka Hamkor Parranda, a private enterprise. Since 2012 we have raised poultry in Asaka district, Andijan region: chicks, chicken meat and eggs.

Our name means “Shuxrat’s son” and carries a family tradition: hard work and honesty passed from father to son. The new look keeps those values.
