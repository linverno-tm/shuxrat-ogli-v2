Questions about chicks
All questions
What is the minimum chick order?
Day-old broiler chicks are sold from 50 birds. Please agree larger batches in advance.
Are the chicks vaccinated?
Yes, chicks are vaccinated at the hatchery. Our specialist will tell you which vaccines were given when you order.
When can I pick up the chicks?
On the agreed date. Leave a request with a date on the website and our specialist will call to agree the day and time.
Do you export chicks?
Yes, day-old chicks are delivered to Afghanistan and Tajikistan.
